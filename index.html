Earning the business by building trust before ever asking for the sale

Logistics Account Executive · Total Quality Logistics

+
Situation

I was responsible for developing new business by prospecting shipping accounts. One prospect I'd been consistently reaching out to wasn't interested because they already had an established logistics provider.

Task

Rather than push for a sale they didn't want, my task was to understand their business well enough to become genuinely useful to them.

Action

I asked questions about their shipping process, priorities, and challenges, and learned that while they were happy with their provider, they sometimes struggled to secure capacity for last-minute shipments. I stayed in touch periodically, shared relevant market updates, and positioned myself as a reliable resource rather than just another salesperson.

Result

When one of their carriers couldn't cover an urgent shipment, they called me: I'd built credibility and stayed engaged. I coordinated the shipment, which opened the door to additional business and reinforced that trust and value come before the ask.

Turning a frustrated customer into a satisfied one, on the spot

Management Trainee · Enterprise Mobility

+
Situation

A customer came in frustrated because the vehicle they had reserved wasn't available.

Task

My task was to ease the tension and find a solution before it turned into an escalated complaint.

Action

I listened calmly and acknowledged their frustration instead of getting defensive, then offered alternatives. I coordinated with the team to secure a better-fit vehicle and kept communication clear throughout.

Result

The customer left satisfied instead of escalating a complaint: a reminder that staying calm and solution-focused under pressure is often the difference between losing a customer and keeping one.

Staying composed and HIPAA-compliant in a high-pressure clinical setting

Patient Transporter · AdventHealth East Orlando

+
Situation

As a Patient Transporter, I regularly moved patients through time-sensitive, high-pressure moments: transporting people who were anxious, in pain, or awaiting urgent procedures, while working alongside nurses and physicians.

Task

I had to keep patient flow moving efficiently for the clinical team without ever compromising patient safety, dignity, or HIPAA compliance.

Action

I communicated clearly and calmly with patients and staff, coordinated timing tightly with nurses and physicians, and treated every handoff with the same discretion regardless of how busy the floor was.

Result

I maintained patient safety and compliance standards across a diverse patient population for a full year: experience that gave me a real, ground-level understanding of hospital workflows and the trust-building skills pharma sales reps need with clinical staff.
